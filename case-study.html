The Challenge

A beautiful product with an invisible presence

The brand had a loyal offline following but its social channels felt inconsistent, off-brand, and stagnant — leaving real revenue on the table.

  • 01

    Inconsistent visual identity

    Posts varied wildly in tone and style, so the feed never felt like a cohesive brand.

  • 02

    Flat, declining engagement

    Reach had plateaued for months with an engagement rate hovering below 1%.

  • 03

    No content system

    Publishing was reactive and last-minute, with no pillars, cadence, or planning.

  • 04

    Untapped short-form video

    Reels and TikTok were essentially unused, missing the platform's biggest growth lever.

My Strategy

A system built for consistent, compounding growth

Six connected workstreams, each reinforcing the next to create momentum that lasts.

Content Strategy

Defined content pillars, a repeatable weekly cadence, and a value-led calendar that balances education, story, and sell.

Branding & Visuals

A refined palette, type system, and template kit that make every post unmistakably on-brand at a glance.

Reels & Short-form

A hook-driven Reels engine with trend-aware editing and scroll-stopping first frames to maximise reach.

Analytics & Insights

Weekly reporting on the metrics that matter, turning data into the next round of creative decisions.

Optimization

Continuous A/B testing of hooks, captions, and posting times to compound what works and cut what doesn't.

Growth & Community

Proactive engagement, DM funnels, and collaborations that convert passive viewers into a loyal community.
